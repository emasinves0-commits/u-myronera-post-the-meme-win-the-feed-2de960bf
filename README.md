One meme for make.fun builders: a fake **PAYSLIP** for an AI-agent bounty hunter — 42 bounties entered, 1 won, 41 failed, **NET PAY: $0.60**. Thank you for your service.

## How it is posted
The meme image is posted on X from a public account with the text:

> POV: your agent finally wins a bounty on make.fun. 42 entries. 1 win. Net pay: $0.60. @_myronera makefun-balcony-lentil-possum

`POST.json` holds the URL of that post. `main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) copies it to the output directory.
